## Osteoporosis Article

**Slug:** `osteoporosis-falls-safe-exercise`

**Working title:** `กระดูกพรุนกับการหกล้ม: ทำไมล้มเบา ๆ จึงกระดูกหักได้ และควรออกกำลังกายอย่างไร`

**Audience:** Older adults and adult children making home-care and physiotherapy decisions.

**Content sequence:**

1. Plain-language definition and why osteoporosis is often silent.
2. Fragility fracture and why a fall from standing height can matter.
3. Warning signs after a fall or possible vertebral fracture that need medical assessment.
4. What safe exercise should develop: strength, balance, functional movement, and suitable weight-bearing activity.
5. Why high impact, sudden excessive loading, and unsuitable spinal movement may need modification for high-risk patients rather than a universal ban on movement.
6. What families can change at home and what a physiotherapist assesses.
7. Clear boundary between self-care, physiotherapy assessment, and medical review.

## Evidence And Safety

- Use current authoritative guidance from WHO fragility fracture and falls resources, NIAMS osteoporosis and exercise guidance, NOGG 2024, and the Royal Osteoporosis Society.
- Do not diagnose osteoporosis from symptoms or from the app.
- Do not promise that exercise alone treats osteoporosis or prevents every fracture.
- Do not tell readers to stop or change prescribed medication.
- Route suspected fracture, new severe back pain after minor trauma, inability to bear weight, deformity, neurological symptoms, or rapid deterioration to medical assessment.
